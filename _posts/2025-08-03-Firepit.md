No longer is our trailer the only one in the Partk without a firepit.

{% img src=/assets/posts/2025-08-03-Firepit/firepit.jpg size="80%" alt="Firepit" align="center"%}

It's not really been a priority for use before as we've never been that good at dodging mosquitoes and smoke.  But our pregnant daughter was coming up and she really likes [S'mores](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/S%27more). So it was time.

Not really a lot going on here, just remove the turf, add a base of sand and lay down the slabs. As you can see from the picture I did a pretty bad job of levelling the slabs.

{% img src=/assets/posts/2025-08-03-Firepit/firepit+trailer.jpg size="80%" alt="Firepit and trailer" align="center"%}

But it was done in time for the visit and it the S'mores were very tasty.
